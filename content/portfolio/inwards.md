## Overview

Kartoza worked with AWARD to develop the next generation of their water resource

management desktop app called ‘InWARDS’. This app shows near real-time stream flow and water

quality data for three water management areas in the Limpopo basin in the northern part of South

Africa. The app is built in Javascript using web technologies but is designed as a desktop application.

Within the app, you can make use of various time-series and map-based visualisations. Data is

retrieved from a web API that is managed by AWARD.
