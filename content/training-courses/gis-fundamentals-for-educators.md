## Course Overview

This hybrid course is designed specifically for teachers and school-level educators who want to bring geospatial thinking into their classrooms. It combines online self-paced modules with facilitated online sessions, making it accessible regardless of where you teach.

You will learn to use QGIS to create maps, work with spatial data, and digitise geographic features — and you will leave with practical, curriculum-aligned lesson resources for Grade 8, 10, and 12 Geography that you can use immediately. No prior GIS experience is needed.

## Course Modules

<div class="course-modules">
  <details class="course-module">
    <summary><span class="course-module-number">1</span><strong>
Getting Started with GIS & QGIS</strong><span class="course-module-time">Online · Self-paced</span></summary>
    <p>Introduction to GIS concepts — what GIS is, why it matters in Geography education, and how QGIS works. Install QGIS, explore the interface, and load your first dataset. Includes a short orientation to the course platform and Communication Hub.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="course-module">
    <summary><span class="course-module-number">2</span><strong>
Working with Spatial Data</strong><span class="course-module-time">Online · Self-paced</span></summary>
    <p>Load and explore vector and raster data. Understand coordinate reference systems in simple terms. Source free South African datasets from OpenStreetMap, Stats SA, and other open repositories. Evaluate data quality for classroom use.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="course-module">
    <summary><span class="course-module-number">3</span><strong>Making Maps for the Classroom</strong><span class="course-module-time">
Facilitated online session</span></summary>
    <p>Style layers, add labels, and produce clear maps using the Print Layout manager. Learn what makes a good classroom map — design principles for learner comprehension — and export maps for printing or digital display. Live session includes Q&A and peer sharing.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="course-module">
    <summary><span class="course-module-number">4</span><strong>Digitising & Field Data Collection</strong><span class="course-module-time">Online · Self-paced</span></summary>
    <p>Create your own geographic features by digitising from aerial images in QGIS. Introduction to field data collection using mobile tools. Practical exercise: create a simple map of your school grounds or local area.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="course-module">
    <summary><span class="course-module-number">5</span><strong>Curriculum-Aligned Lesson Frameworks</strong><span class="course-module-time">
Facilitated online session</span></summary>
    <p>Work through ready-made GIS lesson examples aligned to the Grade 8, 10, and 12 Geography CAPS curriculum. Discuss how to adapt these for your context, integrate them into existing lesson plans, and use GIS as an SBA tool. Participants share their own classroom ideas in a collaborative session.</p>
  </details>
</div>
