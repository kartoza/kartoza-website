## Training Course Cancellation Policy

### A. Cancellation by Participant

If you are unable to attend a training course you've registered for:

- **More than 21 days before the course start date:** You may cancel your registration and receive a full refund or choose to transfer your booking to a future course date (subject to availability).
- **Between 14 days before the course start date:** You are eligible for a **50% refund**, or you may transfer to another course date without penalty.
- **7 days or less before the course start date or no-show:** No refund will be issued. However, **substitutions are allowed** -- you may nominate someone else to attend in your place at no additional cost.

**Note:** All cancellation requests must be submitted in writing to <seabilwe@kartoza.com>.

----

### B. Cancellation by Kartoza

In the event that Kartoza must cancel or reschedule a training course due to low enrolment, instructor availability, or unforeseen circumstances:

- Participants will be offered a full refund, or
- Provided the option to reschedule for a future course date at no additional charge.

**Note:** Kartoza will not be held liable for any travel, accommodation, or other expenses incurred due to a course cancellation.

## Product Cancellation & Refund Policy

### A. Digital Products (e.g. Software, Plugins, Downloads)

Due to the nature of digital products:

- **All sales are considered final.**
- **No refunds** will be issued once a product has been downloaded, accessed, or a license key has been generated and delivered.
- Please ensure that the product meets your requirements before completing the purchase.
- If you experience technical issues, please contact us at <info@kartoza.com> so we can assist.

----

### B. Custom Software Development and Product Services

For custom-developed digital products or solutions (e.g. bespoke plugins, software integrations, or tailored configurations):

- If a client cancels the project after development work has commenced, the client will be invoiced for:
  - All work completed up to the date of cancellation, billed at the agreed hourly or project rate, and
  - Any non-recoverable costs already incurred (e.g. third-party licenses, subscriptions, or contractor fees).
- If cancellation occurs before any development work has started, a cancellation fee may be applicable or agreed upon.
- A detailed timesheet or work report will be provided upon cancellation to support invoicing for time spent.
- We encourage clear communication and planning during project initiation to avoid misunderstandings around cancellations or scope changes.

## How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

To request a cancellation, refund, or course transfer, please contact us at <info@kartoza.com>.

Please include:

- Your full name
- The product/course name
- Date of purchase or registration
- Proof of payment
- Reason for the request
- For a refund, we need a confirmation of the bank account

----

This policy may be updated from time to time. The most current version will always be available on our website.
