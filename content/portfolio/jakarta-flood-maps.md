The Jakarta flood maps project aims to identify and visualise flood-prone areas within the city to improve disaster preparedness and response. 
{{< /block >}}

## Overview

Kartoza developed this application for information gathering and dissemination relating to flooding within the metropolitan area of Jakarta to RW/RT (suburb/block) level. By analysing data on rainfall and topography, the project helps inform urban planning and enhance community resilience against flooding. The platform has a REST API and can generate various reports.
