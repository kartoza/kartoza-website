## Course Overview

This self-paced GIS lesson is designed for Grade 10 Geography learners following the CAPS curriculum. Working at your own pace, you will use QGIS to explore and map tectonic plates, earthquake epicentres, and volcanic activity — connecting core Geography content to real-world spatial data.

The lesson introduces fundamental GIS concepts through hands-on tasks using both raster and vector datasets. It is ideal for SBA task submissions, exam preparation, or as a curriculum enrichment activity that your teacher can use in class.

## What You Will Learn

<div class="materials-grid">
  <div class="material-card">
    <h4>Navigate QGIS</h4>
    <p>Open QGIS, load data layers, and navigate the interface with confidence — no prior GIS experience needed.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="material-card">
    <h4>Work with Raster Data</h4>
    <p>Load and display a world map raster layer and understand how raster data represents continuous geographic information.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="material-card">
    <h4>Work with Vector Data</h4>
    <p>Load and explore vector layers for tectonic plates, earthquake epicentres, and volcanic activity — and understand what each feature represents.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="material-card">
    <h4>Symbolise & Style Layers</h4>
    <p>Apply colours and symbols to distinguish between tectonic plate boundaries, earthquakes, and volcanoes on your map.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="material-card">
    <h4>Identify Spatial Patterns</h4>
    <p>Analyse the spatial distribution of earthquakes and volcanoes relative to tectonic plate boundaries — a key Geography skill.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="material-card">
    <h4>Export a Map Layout</h4>
    <p>Produce a completed, labelled map with a legend, scale bar, and north arrow — ready for SBA submission.</p>
  </div>
</div>
