Wallpapers below are an expanded version of the homepage hero — Kartoza
charcoal canvas, the map motif clipped to a slanted polygon on the right
edge, and the amber slant rule between the two halves.  All sizes carry
the Kartoza logo, our **Open Source Geospatial Experts** strapline, the
vision statement, and **kartoza.com**.

Right-click → _Save image as_… on any thumbnail to download.
