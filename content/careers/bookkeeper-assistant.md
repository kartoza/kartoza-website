We are seeking a Bookkeeper Assistant to support the finance function by assisting with day-to-day bookkeeping and administrative tasks. The successful candidate will work closely with the Bookkeeper to ensure that financial records are accurate, up-to-date, and compliant with company policies. This role involves maintaining financial data, processing transactions, reconciling accounts, and supporting the preparation of reports and audits.  The ideal candidate will be organised, detail-oriented, and able to work independently within a remote, distributed team environment.

  

### RESPONSIBILITIES

#### **General Bookkeeping**

  1. Record and maintain accurate financial transactions in the accounting system.
  2. Maintain the integrity and accuracy of the general ledger.
  3. Support reconciliations of all balance sheet accounts, including bank and petty cash.
  4. Assist with payroll preparation and related record-keeping.
  5. Maintain organised and secure digital filing of financial and accounting documents.
  6. Support the preparation of documentation for auditors, accountants, and management.

#### **Creditors (Accounts Payable)**

  1. Reconcile supplier statements to ensure all invoices are accounted for.
  2. Schedule and process payments to suppliers in accordance with payment terms.
  3. Communicate with suppliers regarding queries, discrepancies, or payment issues.
  4. Maintain accurate supplier records and ensure compliance with internal controls.

#### **Debtors (Accounts Receivable)**

  1. Generate and issue customer invoices accurately and on time.
  2. Record customer receipts and allocate payments to correct accounts.
  3. Monitor and follow up on overdue accounts to ensure timely collection.
  4. Communicate with clients professionally regarding outstanding balances or queries.

  

### EDUCATION AND SKILLS

  1. 2 years of prior bookkeeping experience
  2. Diploma in bookkeeping
