Stratafy empowers small to medium mining and quarrying operations by offering a platform that supports, RPA operations, 2D and 3D imagery, along with robust analysis and reporting tools through comprehensive dashboards.
{{< /block >}}

## Overview

Kartoza built a platform for Stratafy to service small to medium mining and quarrying operations and RPAS operators. RPAS operators can subscribe to upload and process their image products and the resulting orthophotos, point clouds, etc. will then be available on the system to other components and users. Mine operator subscribers have a range of tools at their disposal including environmental, health and safety reports, haul tonnage, blast drill planning, blast volume, backfill volume analysis, safety berms, geology, equipment status, fuel usage, IoT integration, volumetric analysis, rock-face profiling, change over time studies, line of sight analysis, water flow analysis, blast fragmentation, stockpile and tailings analysis. These are available as 2D or 3D spatial layers, reports, dashboards etc.
