## Overview

Remote sensing startup, Swift Geospatial, wanted a tool to allow users to obtain instant quotes for archive or bespoke UAV and satellite imagery and derived products, without waiting for the long quoting turnaround time typical of the industry.

Kartoza developed, hosts and supports this tool, which is a key marketing tool for Swift as well as a revenue generator.
