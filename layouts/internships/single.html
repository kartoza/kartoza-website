What You Need

Minimum Qualifications

We care more about attitude and curiosity than credentials. Don't let the checklist stop you from applying.

Relevant Degree

Geography, GIS, Geoinformatics, Computer Science, Maths, Physics, Remote Sensing, Environmental Sciences, IT, or related. Minimum 3-year undergraduate degree.

Your Own Equipment

A personal computer and reliable high-speed internet. All tools, platform access, and Moodle course materials are provided by us.

Full Commitment

40 hours a week on a flexitime basis. We're fully remote — you manage your schedule as long as you're available to the team.

Curiosity & Drive

A willingness to learn fast, ask questions, and take initiative. The best interns find problems to solve without being asked.

Duration: Minimum 1 Month

Typically 3 months, extended month-by-month if both parties agree. A small monthly stipend is provided throughout.

Exceptions Welcome

School students, school-leavers, and candidates without a degree may be considered at our discretion. Always worth applying

Structured Learning Path

What You'll Learn

Your first week is structured foundation-building on our Moodle platform. From Week 2, you're embedded in real full-industry work.

1
Week 1

Fundamentals of GIS and Mapping

QGIS basics, thematic maps, cartographic design, PostGIS database concepts, GeoNode platform, and OpenStreetMap contribution.

2
Week 1–2

Data Management & Geospatial Development

Computer fundamentals, Python through to vector and raster handling, Shapefile validation, and scripting for geospatial tasks.

3
Weeks 2–3

Automation, Animation & Advanced Applications

Animated maps in QGIS, tourism map creation, and geospatial web development with Django and Folium.

4
Weeks 3–4

Web Mapping & Application Development

GeoNode data platform, publishing datasets, capstone project with a final report and interactive web map deliverable.

5
Ongoing

Infrastructure Mapping

InfrastructureMapper open source project — local setup, GitHub contributions, codebase best practices, and collaborative team workflows.

End of Programme

Course Certificate

Awarded on completion of all modules and the capstone project.

Job shadowing is at our discretion and is not always available. As our team is fully remote, capacity varies — please reach out to check availability before making plans.
