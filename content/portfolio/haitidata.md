## Overview

The GeoNode instance that the World Bank had set up in Haiti a number of years ago to support Disaster Risk Reduction had accumulated many useful data sets but required an upgrade and overhaul. It needed to support clip ‘n ship functionality, handle big data, cope with high load and more. Kartoza subcontracted as GeoNode experts to ITHACA on this World Bank project. We deployed GeoNode with GeoServer backend from our Rancher Catalog and built a clip ‘n ship, tool, among various other components. We also conducted two training sessions In Port au Prince (in French).
