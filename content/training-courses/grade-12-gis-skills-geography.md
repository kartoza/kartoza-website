## Course Overview

This self-paced course consists of four stand-alone GIS extension lessons, each aligned to a topic in the Grade 12 Geography CAPS curriculum. Learners and teachers can work through the lessons independently — at their own pace, at any time — making it ideal for exam preparation, SBA tasks, and supplementary classroom resources.

Each lesson comes with an instructional video, written notes, GIS data, and a marking guide. Together the four lessons cover the full range of Grade 12 Geography case study topics: Climate, Geomorphology, Economic Geography, and Settlement Geography.

## The Four Lessons

<div class="course-modules">
  <details class="course-module">
    <summary><span class="course-module-number">1</span><strong>Climate — GIS Extension Lesson</strong><span class="course-module-time">Self-Paced</span></summary>
    <p>Explore climate data using GIS. Learners work with climate zone layers, temperature and rainfall datasets, and produce maps that illustrate climate patterns. Aligned to the Grade 12 Climate & Weather case study requirements.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="course-module">
    <summary><span class="course-module-number">2</span><strong>Geomorphology — GIS Extension Lesson</strong><span class="course-module-time">Self-Paced</span></summary>
    <p>Use GIS to analyse landforms, elevation data, and drainage patterns. Learners produce terrain maps and interpret geomorphological features relevant to the Grade 12 Geomorphology topic.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="course-module">
    <summary><span class="course-module-number">3</span><strong>Economic Geography — GIS Extension Lesson</strong><span class="course-module-time">Self-Paced</span></summary>
    <p>Map economic activity, infrastructure, and land use data. Learners use GIS to visualise and interpret spatial patterns in economic development, aligned to the Grade 12 Economic Geography case study.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="course-module">
    <summary><span class="course-module-number">4</span><strong>Settlement Geography — GIS Extension Lesson</strong><span class="course-module-time">Self-Paced</span></summary>
    <p>Analyse settlement patterns, population distribution, and urban growth using GIS. Produce maps showing settlement hierarchy and spatial relationships relevant to Grade 12 Settlement Geography.</p>
