## Partnership Overview

Kartoza and LandInfo Technologies have a long-standing partnership centred on **MyCivitas**, a comprehensive asset management platform designed specifically for municipalities and infrastructure organisations. LandInfo Technologies provides the domain expertise in municipal asset management, while Kartoza delivers the software engineering, geospatial development, and cloud infrastructure that powers the platform.

Together, we help municipalities across southern Africa and beyond take control of their infrastructure assets — from roads and water networks to buildings and parks — with a system that is affordable, practical, and built on open source technology.

## What MyCivitas Does

MyCivitas is a web-based asset management system that gives municipalities a clear picture of what they own, what condition it is in, and what it will cost to maintain over time. The platform covers the full asset lifecycle:

### Asset Inventory

MyCivitas provides a structured, map-based inventory of all municipal infrastructure. Every asset — whether a road segment, water pipe, stormwater drain, or community building — is recorded with its location, attributes, condition, and maintenance history. The GIS integration means assets are not just entries in a database but visible, queryable features on a map.

### Condition Assessment

The platform supports systematic condition assessments using standardised rating scales. Field workers use mobile devices to inspect assets and record their current condition, supported by photo documentation and structured assessment forms. Condition data feeds directly into the platform's deterioration models and planning tools.

### Lifecycle Cost Modelling

MyCivitas includes financial modelling tools that project the long-term costs of maintaining, rehabilitating, and replacing infrastructure assets. Municipalities can model different investment scenarios, understand the consequences of deferred maintenance, and build evidence-based capital budgets.

### Risk and Priority Analysis

By combining condition data, criticality ratings, and consequence-of-failure assessments, MyCivitas helps municipalities prioritise their limited maintenance budgets where they will have the greatest impact. High-risk assets that serve critical functions receive appropriate attention.

## How Kartoza Supports the Partnership

### Platform Development

Kartoza is the primary software development partner for MyCivitas. We build and maintain the web application, database architecture, API layer, and user interfaces. The platform is built on Django, PostGIS, and modern frontend technologies, with a strong emphasis on usability and performance.

### Geospatial Engineering

Infrastructure assets are inherently spatial — they exist at specific locations and connect in networks. Kartoza brings deep expertise in geospatial data management, ensuring that MyCivitas handles spatial queries, map rendering, and network analysis efficiently. We integrate with QGIS for advanced spatial analysis and cartographic output.

### Mobile and Field Solutions

We develop the mobile data collection tools that allow field workers to conduct asset inspections and condition assessments on-site. These integrate with Mergin Maps and QField for robust offline-capable field data capture that syncs back to the central MyCivitas database.

### Cloud Infrastructure

Kartoza manages the hosting and DevOps for MyCivitas deployments, ensuring the platform runs reliably and scales to meet the needs of municipalities of all sizes. We handle backups, monitoring, security updates, and performance optimisation.

### Training and Capacity Building

We deliver training programmes for municipal staff — from asset managers and GIS technicians to finance officers and field inspectors — ensuring that each user group can effectively use the parts of the system relevant to their role.

## Who Uses MyCivitas

### Small and Medium Municipalities

MyCivitas is designed to be accessible to municipalities that cannot afford enterprise asset management systems. The open source foundation keeps costs low, while the platform's design focuses on the practical needs of under-resourced local governments.

### District and Regional Authorities

Regional bodies use MyCivitas to aggregate asset data across multiple municipalities, enabling coordinated planning and equitable resource allocation across their jurisdictions.

### Infrastructure Consultants

Engineering and infrastructure consultants use MyCivitas as a platform for delivering asset management services to their municipal clients, including condition assessments, capital planning, and maintenance optimisation.

## Built on Open Source

MyCivitas is built entirely on open source technologies:

- **Django** for the web application framework
- **PostGIS** for spatial database management
- **Leaflet** and **MapLibre** for interactive web mapping
- **QGIS** for advanced analysis and reporting
- **Mergin Maps / QField** for mobile field data collection

This means no licensing fees, no vendor lock-in, and full transparency in how the system works. Municipalities own their data and can extend the system as their needs evolve.
