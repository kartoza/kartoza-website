## Overview

We created a realtime earthquake risk assessment tool. The server automatically generate san impact map within minutes of an earthquake occurring. The tool is deployed in Indonesia where earthquakes are prevalent. It also does realtime flood analyses for Jakarta and volcanic ash analyses.
