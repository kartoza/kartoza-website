## Partnership Overview

Kartoza and BGEO have built a partnership centred on Giswater, the open source platform for integrated water cycle management. BGEO provides the deep domain expertise in water supply, sewerage, and urban drainage, while Kartoza delivers the cloud infrastructure, GIS engineering, and support services that bring the platform to utilities worldwide. Together, we help water utilities across Spain, Portugal, Latin America, Africa, and beyond take control of their network assets and operational data, with a system that is affordable, practical,and built entirely on open-source technology.

## What BGEO Does

BGEO is a knowledge-driven open-source GIS and water solutions company based in Granollers, Barcelona. Founded by water and GIS engineers, BGEO develops and maintains Giswater, the open-source platform connecting GIS, hydraulic models, and spatial databases for water network management. BGEO is ISO 9001 certified and a co-founder of the Giswater Association, which coordinates the global open-source community around the platform.

With clients across Spain, Portugal, Latin America, and Africa, BGEO brings deep domain expertise in water supply, sewerage, urban drainage, and municipal GIS, combined with a commitment to open-source technology that removes barriers to entry for utilities of all sizes.

## From Data to Decision in Three Steps

### 1. Migrate & Model

We migrate your existing network data into a structured PostGIS database with BGEO's validated schema , complete with asset catalogues, topology rules, and hydraulic model linkage. Your network is clean, connected, and analysis-ready from day one.

### 2. Deploy on Geospatial Hosting

Kartoza provides your Giswater environment on Geospatial Hosting in your preferred region. Within days you have a fully configured cloud instance, QGIS desktop sessions, a web client, PostGIS backend, and Moodle training portal, all in one place.

### 3. Operate & Grow

Your team manages the network through the QGIS desktop or browser client. Kartoza handles infrastructure and first-line support; BGEO provides application and domain expertise. As your needs grow, extend the platform with hydraulic modelling, IoT, or advanced analytics.

## Real-world Impact

BGEO's Giswater platform is active in over 140 countries, serving water utilities from small villages of 2,000 inhabitants to regional operators managing hundreds of  thousands of connections. In Costa Rica, the Instituto Costarricense de Acueductos y Alcantarillado deployed Giswater as their standard national GIS platform, the first country-level rollout of its kind, replacing fragmented tools with a unified open source environment for network inventory, hydraulic modelling, and asset management.

In Brazil, Saneago, one of the country's largest sanitation companies, modernised itssewerage management using Giswater, integrating network GIS with operational workflows and improving data quality across thousands of kilometres of infrastructure.
