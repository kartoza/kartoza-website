## Overview

Kartoza built this web application whose objective is to help school leavers explore and choose components of a career path that suits their goals, budget and location. It started off with rich information about most types of courses available in the Western Cape Province and is progressing to populate national data from organisations like SAQA (South African National Qualifications Authority).
