## Overview

National Geo-Spatial Information (NGI), South Africa’s national mapping agency, supplies free

topographical data to the public, yet they do not supply the map styles. Users who want a

topographical map sheet needed a paper one or a scanned digital version. Kartoza's Topostyle project

is an attempt to emulate the official styling with QGIS and SLD styles and map compositions.
