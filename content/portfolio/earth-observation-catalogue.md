Kartoza was tasked with building a data catalogue for SANSA (South African National Space Agency) that the public can search for and order products through.
{{< /block >}}

## Overview

SANSA tasks satellites to acquire imagery and provides a huge catalogue of data which they make available for free and to purchase to the general public. The public can search for and order products via a web based data catalogue. We built the data catalogue and provide ongoing support development services for the catalogue.
