Suzanne has been the backbone of Kartoza's financial administration since 2016,
bringing nearly a decade of institutional knowledge and dedication to the role.

As Financial Administrator, Suzanne manages the critical financial operations
that keep Kartoza running smoothly.

Her long tenure with Kartoza means she has deep understanding of the company's
financial rhythms and requirements. This institutional knowledge is invaluable
for ensuring consistent, accurate financial management through various projects
and growth phases.

Suzanne's role, while often behind the scenes, is essential to Kartoza's
success. By ensuring that vendors are paid on time, invoices are processed
correctly, and team members receive their compensation accurately, she enables
everyone else to focus on their core work.

Based in Australia, Suzanne works closely with the leadership team to
maintain financial health and compliance. Her professionalism and consistency
have made her a trusted member of Kartoza's administrative team.
